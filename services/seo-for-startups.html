FAQ Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common queries about our SEO Services for Startups services.

How long does SEO take to show results?

For new startup domains, it typically takes 3 to 6 months to see meaningful organic traffic growth. SEO is a long-term compounding investment. The pages we optimize today build authority that continues to deliver leads for years.

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No honest SEO agency can guarantee a specific ranking on Google, as search algorithms update constantly. We focus on search best practices: fast load times, semantic HTML, schema markup, high-quality content, and secure domain authority.

What is included in a technical SEO audit?

A technical audit checks site health issues that prevent search crawlers from index-matching your pages. We audit your robots.txt, XML sitemap, SSL configuration, Core Web Vitals, 301/404 redirect status, and schema markup.

Do you write content too, or just optimize?

We do both! We research keywords, map content structures, write optimized copy, and help publish posts. We also optimize your team's draft content to ensure it ranks.

Is local SEO worth it if I sell online only?

If you operate a global SaaS or pure e-commerce storefront, traditional local SEO is less critical. However, local optimizations can still capture high-intent traffic in regional tech hubs, while local schema data builds general trust with Google.
