FAQ Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common queries about our Affordable Web Development services.

How much does a startup website cost?

Our startup website development starts at $1,500 for a marketing site and goes up based on complexity. We build a clear roadmap showing exactly where every dollar is spent, ensuring you pay only for features that drive user growth and conversions.

How long does it take to build a website?

A custom marketing website takes between 2 to 4 weeks from kickoff to launch. More complex web applications, software MVPs, or full headless e-commerce builds can take 6 to 10 weeks, depending on integration requirements and features.

Can you migrate my existing site without downtime?

Yes. We build and test your new website on a secure staging server. Once it is fully approved, we handle the domain transition, database migration, and URL redirections during off-peak hours, ensuring zero disruption to your active users.

Do you build on WordPress or custom code?

We build on both! The choice depends entirely on your needs. For content-heavy sites needing daily edits, we recommend a hardened, custom WordPress CMS. For custom SaaS apps, interactive tools, or web services, we build custom React/Node.js code.

Will my site be mobile-friendly and fast?

Absolutely. Every site we build is mobile-first, fully responsive, and highly optimized for speed. We test against Google Lighthouse Core Web Vitals to guarantee fast load times, which boosts user experience and search engine optimization (SEO) performance.

Do you offer maintenance after launch?

Yes, we provide 30 days of free support post-launch to fix any bugs. We also offer affordable monthly maintenance plans that cover regular security updates, backup monitoring, speed optimization, and content updates.
