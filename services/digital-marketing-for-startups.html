FAQ Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common queries about our Digital Marketing services.

What is the minimum ad budget needed to see results?

We recommend starting with at least $500 to $1,000 per month in direct ad spend. This budget allows the ad algorithms to test copy, target profiles, and gather enough data to optimize conversions.

Do you manage social media posting or just advertising?

We focus on paid customer acquisition (social media advertising, PPC, email flows) and general growth strategy rather than daily organic social posting. We can, however, write social copy templates.

How is this different from your SEO service?

SEO is an organic search strategy that takes months to rank but builds permanent, free traffic. Digital marketing (PPC, Paid Social) is a paid strategy that generates leads instantly but stops delivering the moment the budget is cut.

Can you run campaigns on a $500/month budget?

Yes, we optimize small budgets by focusing on niche keywords and tight audience segments. This keeps click costs low and avoids wasting money on broad keywords.
