FAQ Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common queries about our Startup Web Design services.

What's the difference between web design and web development?

Web design focuses on the visual aesthetics, layout structure, typography, brand colors, and user experience (UX) of your site, usually built in Figma. Web development is the coding phase where developers write HTML, CSS, and JS to turn that static design into a live, interactive website.

How many revisions are included in the design phase?

We include two complete rounds of visual revisions. We present high-fidelity mockups in Figma, gather your structured feedback, and refine the interface. This ensures the final layout perfectly reflects your branding and expectations.

Do you provide the Figma files?

Yes. Upon project completion, we hand over full access to the organized Figma source files, including all UI design systems, components, grids, fonts, and assets. You have total ownership and can use them for future mockups.

Can you redesign my site without losing my SEO rankings?

Absolutely. During a redesign, we work closely with our technical SEO team to map out your current URL structure, maintain important content sections, optimize headings, and create a 301 redirection plan. This keeps your search authority safe.

Do you design landing pages for ad campaigns specifically?

Yes, we specialize in high-converting landing page designs tailored for Google, Meta, or LinkedIn ad campaigns. We structure these layouts with single CTAs, persuasive copy blocks, and speed-optimized designs to maximize your return on ad spend (ROAS).
