FAQ Common Questions

Got questions? Here are answers to the most common ones about our affordable services for startups.

What makes your services affordable for startups?

We use efficient workflows, modern technology, and smart automation to reduce development time and overhead costs. This lets us deliver agency-quality work at startup-friendly prices — without compromising on quality or security.

Do you work with early-stage startups?

Absolutely! We specialize in helping early-stage startups and emerging businesses build their digital presence from the ground up. Whether you need an MVP, a marketing website, or full-stack development — we tailor our services to your stage and budget.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary by project scope. Simple websites can be delivered in 5–7 days. More complex web applications or custom software typically take 2–6 weeks. We provide clear timelines and milestones before starting any project.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes! We offer flexible support packages including server maintenance, security updates, performance monitoring, and content management. We're your long-term digital partner, not a one-time vendor.

Can you help with both design and development?

Yes — we offer end-to-end solutions. From UI/UX design and branding to full-stack development, SEO, digital marketing, and cybersecurity. You get a single, affordable partner for all your digital needs.
